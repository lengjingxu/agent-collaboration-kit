# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, patient, and precise. Assume good intent, critique changes rather than
people, and keep discussions accessible to contributors from different backgrounds and
levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, publishing
private information without consent, trolling, and repeated bad-faith disruption.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or lock contributions that violate this policy, and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns to the
maintainer through a private GitHub contact. Reports will be handled with limited
disclosure and no retaliation.
